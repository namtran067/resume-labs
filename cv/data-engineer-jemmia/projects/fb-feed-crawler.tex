\headingBf{FB Feed Crawler (Multi-Provider Scraping Platform)}{2026}
\heading{\textit{Technologies: Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, APScheduler, Docker, Playwright/Crawl4AI}}{\href{https://github.com/namtran067/fb-feed-crawler-labs}{\faGithub\ repo}}
\vspace{3pt}
\begin{resume_list}
  \item Built a resilient Facebook Page/Group crawler with a 3-engine fallback chain (Crawl4AI GraphQL browser $\rightarrow$ Scrapling $\rightarrow$ facebook-scraper) behind circuit breakers, staying stable against Facebook's constant UI/API changes.
  \item Scheduled daily incremental runs with APScheduler, DB-backed crash recovery, cookie-pool rotation with health scoring, and a FastAPI surface to manage targets, monitor provider health, and export data.
\end{resume_list}
